# Acceptance Criteria
The platform must not visually copy prior prototypes. Navigation must scale. Every KPI must drill down. Workbench must be role-aware. Exceptions are first-class records. Audit timeline appears on material records.

Subvention must enforce unique IMEI, duplicate-claim prevention, filing timelines, maker-checker segregation, approved-batch locking, transaction-level OEM response, approved-value invoicing, partial receipt allocation and reconciled closure.
