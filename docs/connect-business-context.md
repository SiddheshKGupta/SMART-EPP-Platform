# Connect Business Context
Smart EPP is an employer-anchored B2B2E device leasing programme. The employer is the contractual obligor and the employee is the beneficiary. Connect underwrites the employer.

Current operations use SaaS, Excel trackers, GSTZENT, Tally, bank portals, email and shared drives. The institutionalisation objective is to convert SOPs, trackers, controls and governance into one workflow-driven and auditable platform.

Key risks include multiple sources of truth, manual handoffs, sanction/lot utilisation gaps, GST/location errors, subvention leakage, foreclosure billing delays and weak evidence retention.
