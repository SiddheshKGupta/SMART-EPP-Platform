# Platform Vision
Smart EPP is one enterprise platform, not three unrelated applications.

## Initial modules
- Onboarding & Programme Readiness
- Foreclosure & Lease Closure
- Subvention Management

## Shared layer
Employer, employee, OEM, distributor, reseller, product, lease and lot masters; workflow engine; RBAC; exceptions; notifications; documents; audit trail; reporting; integration adapters.

## User outcomes
Management sees attention, blockers, deadlines, financial impact and ownership. Operational users land on personalised work, approvals and exception queues.
