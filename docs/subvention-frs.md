# Smart EPP FRS — Subvention Management
## Objective
Digitally manage OEM subvention from scheme configuration through claim preparation, approval, OEM response, invoicing, collection, accounting, reconciliation, MIS and closure.

## Flow
OEM Scheme → Employer Programme → Purchase Transactions → Eligibility → Claim Batch → Maker Review → Checker Approval → OEM Submission → OEM Response → Rejection/Representation → Invoice → Collection → Accounting → Reconciliation → Closure.

## Functional areas
OEM, distributor, reseller, scheme, employer programme and product masters; purchase repository; rule engine; claim preparation; approval; submission; response; rejection; invoice; collection; accounting; reconciliation; exceptions; MIS; documents; audit; notifications; reports.

## Critical rules
Unique OEM codes; effective-dated schemes; overlap validation; exactly one applicable scheme/programme; unique IMEI; no duplicate claims; configurable filing timeline; system-calculated values; immutable approved batches; transaction-level OEM response; preserved rejection history; invoice against approved value; closure only after reconciliation.
